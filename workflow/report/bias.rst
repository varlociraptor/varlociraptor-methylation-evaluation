Number of CpG loci with a bias called by Varlociraptor, by sequencing technology, at the FDR level α shown in the title.
Panels show Illumina with all samples pooled (loci counted per sample), PacBio and Nanopore.
Each locus is counted once, even if the bias was called in both replicates. Varlociraptor calls at most one bias per
replicate; if the replicates have different bias types, the type of replicate 1 is shown.
We distinguish between loci where the bias was called in both replicates, the other replicate had an alteration fraction (AF, here methylation rate) of 0, or an alteration fraction of greater than zero.
