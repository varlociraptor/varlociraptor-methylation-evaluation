Left:
Strategy for identifying loci with high or low methylation that are not classified as fully methylated or unmethylated. We only use loci at which pb-CpG-tools reported a very low (< 5, top) or very high (> 95, bottom) methylation rate in at least one replicate. The shape indicates the classification: loci are labeled 'low' if Varlociraptor's methylation value equals $0$ (top) or is below $90$ (bottom), and 'high' if it exceeds $10$ (top) or equals $100$ (bottom). Loci with intermediate Varlociraptor values are excluded, as they do not clearly support either classification. Red dashed lines mark the thresholds used to select candidate loci.

Right:
Relative frequency
of methylation rates at the loci selected in A, called by Varlociraptor across all other Illumina and Nanopore samples.
The x-axis shows methylation level binned in increments of 5pp, the y-axis shows the relative proportion of calls in each bin. Colors correspond to the ‘low’/‘high’ classification derived from the Varlociraptor call on the PacBio sample.
