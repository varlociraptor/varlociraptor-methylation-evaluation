Calibration of Varlociraptor's FDR control on simulated data.
A CpG site is called *highly methylated* (event HIGH: methylation rate > 50%, see scenario) if its posterior probability of not being HIGH is at most the FDR threshold α (x-axis).
The y-axis shows the true FDR, i.e. the fraction of these calls whose simulated methylation rate is actually ≤ 50%.
Curves are stratified by simulated read coverage.
Points on or below the dashed diagonal indicate that the FDR is controlled at the requested level; points above it indicate that the posterior probabilities are overconfident.
